Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026. Your trust matters more than our marketing.

1. What we collect

When you contact us, we collect the details you share — your name, email, company and message. Our website also collects basic, anonymised analytics (pages visited, device type) to improve the site. We do not collect sensitive personal data through this website.

2. How we use it

We use your details solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals and deliver our services. We never sell your data, and we never share it with third parties for marketing. Analytics data is aggregated and cannot identify you.

3. Confidentiality in engagements

Client business information shared during diagnostics and engagements is treated as confidential by default. We sign NDAs on request before any deep-dive, and access is limited to the consultants working on your project.

4. Data retention

Enquiry records are kept for up to 24 months so we can follow up meaningfully. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to hello@qrtconsulting.com, and we will comply within 30 days.

5. Cookies

This site uses a minimal set of cookies for core functionality and anonymised analytics. No advertising trackers, no cross-site profiling.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact hello@qrtconsulting.com for any privacy request.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and highlight material changes on this page.
